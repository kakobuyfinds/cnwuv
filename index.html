From browsing to a better decision

How to use an OopBuy spreadsheet

An OopBuy spreadsheet is a product research list: it brings names, links, categories and comparison notes together. A useful row helps you identify the exact item and the details still needing confirmation.

1

Identify the exact option

Search a product type, then record the listing reference, size and color. Similar photographs can represent different versions, materials or quantities. Keep the original source beside your notes.

2

Check fit and evidence

Compare actual measurements using consistent units. If you use a shopping agent, ask what inspection photos and measurements are available for your order. Catalog photos alone do not establish condition or authenticity.

3

Confirm the complete cost

Check whether your chosen agent accepts the listing, then review its current fees and shipping quote. Record unknown charges separately and confirm return conditions before paying.

Useful answers

OopBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this an official OopBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. Its catalog selection comes from CAFANS, and its shopping buttons open CAFANS. This page does not process payments, manage OopBuy accounts or arrange shipping.

Are these real products from the CAFANS catalog?

The displayed names, image URLs, reference numbers and estimated prices were imported from the CAFANS homepage selection checked on November 19, 2026. This is a static selection rather than a live stock feed. Confirm the current listing before deciding.

Can every CAFANS item be ordered through OopBuy?

That has not been verified. Find the product on CAFANS, review its available source or agent options, and check acceptance with your chosen agent before paying. A directory listing does not guarantee that an agent can purchase or ship it.

Do the displayed prices include international shipping?

The amounts shown are estimated item prices in USD. This page does not quote delivery, service fees or taxes. Use current information from the seller and your chosen agent to build a complete budget.

Are the product images QC photos?

No. These are catalog images from the source listings. If inspection is available through your chosen agent, review photos of the actual ordered item and request measurements or close-ups where needed.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Measure a garment that fits you, then compare equivalent chest, shoulder, sleeve and body-length measurements with the selected listing. Check whether the measurements refer to a flat garment or body circumference and keep the units consistent.

How do I find more items?

Open CAFANS using either main button. Search the product name or reference shown on a card, or browse its wider catalog. Filters on this page cover only the eight imported references.
